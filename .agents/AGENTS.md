# Project Rules - BabyClothing

This project is a premium e-commerce platform for baby and kids clothing. Always adhere to the following design and communication guidelines to keep the site highly appealing to parents and families:

## 1. Visual Design & Tone
- **Parents & Kids Friendly**: The aesthetic should be warm, trustworthy, gentle, and welcoming. Avoid cold, overly corporate, or clinical designs.
- **Color Palette & Visuals**: Keep colors curated, warm, and comforting (neutral tones, cozy creams, warm peach/terracotta, soft luxury gold highlights).
- **Interactive Elements**: Use soft, playful micro-animations (like hover wiggles, heartbeat icons, and staggered slide-ins) to create a pleasant and engaging experience for parents browsing the catalog.
- **Typography & Details**: Keep text clear, highly readable, and friendly (rounded UI elements, soft border radii, and premium Outfit typography).
